# Chat History - User Guide

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

### 1️⃣ View Your Chat History
1. Sign in to your account
2. Click the **"History"** tab
3. Click **"🔄 Refresh"** to load your conversations

### 2️⃣ Load a Previous Chat
1. **Double-click** any chat in the list
   - OR -
2. **Select** a chat and click **"📖 Load Chat"**

### 3️⃣ Continue the Conversation
1. Type your follow-up question in the chat box
2. Click **"Send"**
3. The AI remembers the previous context!

## Common Questions

### Q: How many chats can I see?
**A:** The last 50 conversations. This is enough for most users and loads quickly.

### Q: Can I search for specific chats?
**A:** Not yet, but it's coming! For now, scroll through the list or note the timestamps.

### Q: What happens if I ask a question without loading a chat?
**A:** It's treated as a new conversation with no previous context.

### Q: Can I continue a conversation multiple times?
**A:** Yes! Each time you load a chat, you can ask one follow-up. To continue further, load the new chat that was just created.

### Q: Does the AI remember everything?
**A:** The AI receives the previous question and the first 200 characters of the response as context. This is usually enough for good continuity.

### Q: Can I see all my conversations with one click?
**A:** Yes! Just go to History tab and click 🔄 Refresh.

### Q: What if I delete a chat by accident?
**A:** Deletions are permanent. A confirmation dialog helps prevent accidents, but there's no undo after confirmation.

## Tips & Tricks

### 💡 Tip 1: Use Double-Click
Double-clicking a chat is the fastest way to load it. No need to click Load button!

### 💡 Tip 2: Keep Context Fresh
After asking a follow-up, the context clears. If you want to continue again, go back to History and load the newest chat.

### 💡 Tip 3: Regular Cleanup
Delete old test chats or sensitive conversations to keep your history organized.

### 💡 Tip 4: Review Before Continuing
Always read the loaded conversation before asking a follow-up. This ensures your question makes sense in context.

### 💡 Tip 5: Check Timestamps
Chats are sorted by date (newest first). Look at timestamps to find specific conversations.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: History tab is empty
**Solutions:**
1. Click "🔄 Refresh" button
2. Make sure you're signed in
3. Check if you have any previous chats

---

### Problem: Can't load a chat
**Solutions:**
1. Make sure the chat is selected (highlighted)
2. Try double-clicking instead
3. Click "📖 Load Chat" button

---

### Problem: Follow-up doesn't include context
**Check:**
1. Did you load the chat first?
2. Look for "💬 Continuing..." message
3. Context is only used once per load

---

### Problem: Delete button doesn't work
**Solutions:**
1. Make sure a chat is selected
2. Check internet connection
3. Try refreshing and selecting again

---

### Problem: Chats not appearing after sending
**Solutions:**
1. Wait 2-3 seconds for sync
2. Click "🔄 Refresh" manually
3. Check console for errors

## Status Messages Explained

| ✅ Chat deleted successfully | Chat removed from history |

| ❌ Error... | Something went wrong (check details) |

## Best Practices

### ✅ DO
- Refresh history regularly to see new chats
- Review loaded chat before asking follow-ups
- Delete sensitive conversations
- Use double-click for speed
- Read timestamps to find specific chats

### ❌ DON'T
- Don't expect context to persist across multiple questions
- Don't load multiple chats in sequence (context overwrites)
- Don't forget to confirm deletions carefully
- Don't expect immediate sync (wait 2-3 seconds)
